Postdoctoral Researcher | UNC Chapel Hill

The Scales of Polar Oceans.

I connect millimeter-scale ocean movement to geophysical flows and ice sheet behavior. I'm a physical oceanographer and glaciologist studying how the ocean melts ice, from meltwater plumes rising along glacier faces to warm water reaching Antarctica's grounding zones.

My current work combines high-resolution, GPU-accelerated ocean models with laboratory experiments, using particle image velocimetry and high-speed cameras to see the flow right at the ice. Together, they show how energy in the ocean is converted into fluxes of momentum, heat, and salt at glacier boundaries.
