Mentoring students is one of the most rewarding parts of my work, both in research and beyond it.

## Research mentorship

**UNC Sea Ice Project** · Undergraduate Research Mentor · UNC Chapel Hill  
I mentor an undergraduate researcher at UNC Chapel Hill on a sea ice research project.

**REU Mentor** · Summer 2024  
I worked with an undergraduate from Spelman College exploring ICESat-2 elevation data across Antarctic ice shelf termini, building an automatic system for detecting "rampart–moat" structures at the ice shelf edge. Along the way, the student learned ice sheet science, remote sensing, and Python.

**Wave Erosion and Polynyas** · Undergraduate Research Mentor · Georgia Tech  
I mentored Georgia Tech undergraduates on projects combining observations and computational modeling, including ocean surface wave erosion of icebergs and polynyas, with hands-on guidance in research methods, data analysis, and science communication.
