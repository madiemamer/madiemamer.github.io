Mentoring students and sharing ice–ocean science beyond the university are a core part of my work.

## Research mentoring

**REU Mentor** · Summer 2024  
I worked with an undergraduate from Spelman College exploring ICESat-2 elevation data across Antarctic ice shelf termini, building an automatic system for detecting "rampart–moat" structures at the ice shelf edge. Along the way, the student learned ice sheet science, remote sensing, and Python.

**Undergraduate Research Mentor** · Fall 2023  
I mentored a Georgia Tech undergraduate on a project combining observations and computational modeling to understand ocean surface wave erosion of icebergs, with hands-on guidance in research methods, data analysis, and science communication.

**Progress Mentor** · Fall 2022  
I mentored a young woman in STEM from the greater Atlanta area, offering guidance on academic and professional development and helping build confidence and leadership skills.

## Outreach and teaching

**Sea-level rise curriculum** · Summers 2024 & 2025  
Through Georgia Tech's GIFT program, I worked with Dr. Alex Robel and an Atlanta high school math teacher to design a standards-aligned sea-level rise curriculum built on tide gauge records and NOAA's global sea-level data.

**STEM Professional School Partnership** · 2021–2022  
With the Robel and Chu labs, I designed and taught a middle school lesson on geologic time and Earth's history.

**Teaching** · Guest lectures on ice–ocean interactions and scientific computing; teaching assistant for Earth System Modeling and a math and coding practical. *Outstanding Graduate Teaching Assistantship Award, Georgia Tech.*
